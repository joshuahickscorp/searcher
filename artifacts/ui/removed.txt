What left the first screen, and where it lives now

Removed from the first screen
- Tagline “Find the exact item, not merely something similar.”
  Dropped from the header. Product voice, not a legal obligation.
- Header links Privacy and Limitations
  One-line footer: “Not a professional authenticator. Privacy · Limitations”
- Dropzone paragraph and “1 to 10 raster images…”
  Hint is now “Drop, click, or paste.” The 1–10 constraint remains in a
  visually-hidden description and in the unchanged validation messages.
- Tag helper paragraph (“Comma or Enter…”)
  Visually hidden; still tied to the tag field via aria-describedby.
- Long results disclaimer defining Real / Possibly Real
  Compressed to one line in the drawer. Full definitions added to #/limitations.
  Tab subtitles still state what each list means.

Not deleted
- #/privacy and #/limitations routes and their original content.
- Client-side validation messages (wording unchanged).
- Honest empty / blocked handling: coverage, per-source outcomes, stated reason.
- Real and Possibly Real lists (Replica is a third list, never merged).

Moved onto #/limitations
- Searcher is not a professional authenticator (already there).
- What Real and Possibly Real mean (added).
- Replica sources are searched to find replicas; a replica listing can never
  be ranked Real (added).
